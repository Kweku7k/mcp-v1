Monkeys are primates, not apes — they typically have tails.
There are over 260 species of monkeys, split into Old World and New World groups.
Capuchin monkeys are known for using tools, like cracking nuts with stones.
Howler monkeys are one of the loudest land animals — their calls can travel 3 miles.
Mandrills are the world's largest monkeys, with males having brightly colored faces.
Pygmy marmosets are the smallest monkeys, small enough to fit in a human hand.
Spider monkeys use their prehensile tails almost like a fifth limb for swinging through trees.
Snow monkeys (Japanese macaques) are famous for bathing in hot springs during winter.
Rhesus macaques were the first primates sent into space by the US, in 1948.
Some monkeys, like vervet monkeys, use distinct alarm calls for different predators.
